Sky West Real Estate Services, Inc. (herein referred to as Sky West) was formed in 2004 in Los Angeles, CA

The name was envisioned by Jeffrey Lowden, the Founder, when the Internet was just being formed and born. The vision was SKY being BIG VISION BIG SKY and WEST being a Development, Brokerage and Management company in every major CITY on the West Coast.

Sky West was formed originally as Sky West Brokerage, Inc. then changed to encompass more than just Brokerage because Sky West ALL Services in commercial real estate. Since the formation of the company Sky West has gone on to grow in Development, Entitlement, Brokerage, Property Management, Tenant Representation and Construction and Construction Management services.

## What Is Design Development?

Design development (DD) takes the schematic design (SD) one step further. Our architects take your input from the SD phase and translate it into a more detailed building plan. Within design development, we make final selections on specific products and furnishings, while preliminary engineering begins on the building’s various systems.

By the end of the design development stage, you will have a fully-fleshed blueprint ready to submit for permits and begin the bidding process.

### Architect-Directed Product Selection

A significant portion of design development is working with the client to make product selections. Our architects offer a pragmatic perspective during this task, and our guidance ensures that the project budget stays on track.

Allow us to assist you in exploring all the amenities available to you. More than that, allow us to help you analyze each one’s longevity, energy efficiency, value, and beauty. In design development, you can expect to give the final say on:

-   Flooring
-   Windows
-   Doors
-   Appliances
-   And more

This is an exciting time where you have free reign to add your personal flair to all these features. We’ll work closely with you to make sure every choice is judicious and will serve your best interests well into the future.

Upon DD completion, you’ll have a perfected:

## Industrial Remains Strong While Investors Position for Long-Term Growth

The first half of 2026 has reinforced what many in the commercial real estate industry have anticipated for years: Northern Nevada continues to be one of the strongest commercial markets in the western United States.

Driven by population growth, strategic logistics advantages, manufacturing expansion, and continued investment in infrastructure, the Reno-Sparks region remains attractive to business owners, developers, investors, and national tenants alike.

While headlines often focus on interest rates or national market uncertainty, commercial real estate remains a local business. Here in Northern Nevada, market fundamentals continue to support healthy long-term growth. At Sky West Real Estate Services, we believe understanding _why_ the market is changing is just as important as knowing _what_ is happening.

_**Here's our mid-year look at the commercial real estate landscape.**_

![sky-west-logo](https://skywestservices.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/1170/2026/07/sky-west-logo.png)

## Industrial Market Continues to Lead

Industrial real estate remains the strongest asset class throughout Northern Nevada.

Demand for warehouse, manufacturing, distribution, contractor yards, industrial outdoor storage (IOS), and flex space continues to outpace available inventory in many submarkets.

Northern Nevada's location—serving California while offering a more business-friendly operating environment—continues attracting logistics companies, advanced manufacturers, e-commerce distributors, and regional service providers.

While speculative development has added new inventory over the past several years, quality industrial buildings with functional layouts, modern loading capabilities, and well-located industrial land continue to see strong interest.

Developers are also increasingly seeking shovel-ready sites where projects can move quickly through construction.

## What we're seeing:

-   Continued demand for industrial land
-   Increased interest in build-to-suit developments
-   Strong activity in contractor yards and industrial outdoor storage
-   Stable lease rates despite additional inventory
-   Investors continuing to pursue quality industrial assets

## Office Market Stabilizing

Office has been one of the most discussed sectors nationally, but Northern Nevada continues to outperform many larger metropolitan markets. Companies are becoming more intentional about how they use office space rather than simply reducing square footage. Businesses want flexible layouts, modern finishes, collaborative work environments, and locations that help attract employees.

Professional medical offices, financial institutions, engineering firms, legal offices, and service-based businesses continue to support steady office demand throughout Reno and Sparks. Owners who invest in modernizing older office properties remain well-positioned to compete.

## Retail Benefits from Population Growth

Retail fundamentals remain healthy throughout Northern Nevada.

As residential development continues across Reno, Sparks, Spanish Springs, and surrounding communities, retailers continue following rooftops. Neighborhood shopping centers anchored by grocery stores, fitness operators, restaurants, healthcare providers, and service-oriented businesses continue generating consistent leasing activity.

Consumers continue favoring convenience, creating demand for locations with strong visibility, easy access, and ample parking. Inline retail centers and mixed-use developments remain particularly attractive to both local businesses and expanding regional brands.

## Investment Activity Remains Selective

Commercial investment activity has become increasingly disciplined during 2026.

Investors remain active but are placing greater emphasis on cash flow, tenant quality, lease stability, and long-term appreciation rather than simply chasing aggressive cap rates. vIndustrial properties continue attracting the highest level of investor demand, while well-located retail centers with strong tenant mixes remain highly desirable.

Many buyers are also looking beyond stabilized assets and evaluating redevelopment opportunities where strategic improvements can unlock additional value. Northern Nevada continues attracting both local investors and institutional capital looking for long-term market stability.

![Brad](https://skywestservices.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/1170/2026/07/brad1.jpg)

## Construction Pipeline Reflects Long-Term Confidence

Although financing costs remain higher than several years ago, construction activity throughout Northern Nevada continues to demonstrate confidence in the region's future.

Ground-up industrial facilities, retail centers, mixed-use developments, infrastructure improvements, and tenant improvement projects remain active throughout the market. Businesses are increasingly recognizing that waiting for "perfect" market conditions often means missing opportunities. Strategic development continues because Northern Nevada's long-term economic drivers remain intact.

At Sky West Real Estate Services, we work closely with our sister company, Biltmore Construction, allowing clients to evaluate both the real estate opportunity and the construction feasibility before making major investment decisions. Whether it's land acquisition, site preparation, tenant improvements, or ground-up commercial development, bringing brokerage and construction expertise together often creates more efficient projects and better long-term outcomes.

![Biltmore](https://skywestservices.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/1170/2026/07/biltmore-STICKER-logo.png)

## Market Outlook for the Second Half of 2026

**Looking ahead, we remain optimistic.**

Northern Nevada continues benefiting from several long-term economic advantages:

-   Strategic access to West Coast markets
-   Business-friendly tax environment
-   Continued population growth
-   Expanding manufacturing sector
-   Distribution and logistics advantages
-   Ongoing infrastructure investment
-   Strong entrepreneurial activity

While interest rates, construction costs, and national economic conditions will continue influencing transaction volume, the underlying fundamentals supporting Northern Nevada commercial real estate remain exceptionally strong.

**Businesses continue relocating.**

**Developers continue investing.**

**Investors continue seeking opportunities.**

And commercial users continue expanding throughout the region.

## What This Means for Buyers, Sellers, Landlords, and Tenants

**Market conditions continue rewarding preparation.**

For sellers, proper pricing and strategic marketing remain essential.

For buyers, understanding future development corridors may create significant long-term value.

For landlords, investing in property improvements can increase tenant demand and lease performance.

For tenants, working with experienced commercial brokers early in the site selection process often results in better locations, stronger lease negotiations, and fewer surprises during build-outs.

Every commercial real estate decision should be supported by market knowledge, financial analysis, and a long-term strategy.

## Partner With Local Market Experts

Commercial real estate is local, and no one understands Northern Nevada's evolving commercial landscape better than professionals actively working in it every day.

Whether you're buying, selling, leasing, investing, or planning a new commercial development, Sky West Real Estate Services provides the market insight, strategic guidance, and industry relationships needed to help you make informed decisions. # Why Commercial Real Estate and Commercial Construction Go Hand in Hand

Sky West Real Estate Services, Inc. Admin

July 14, 2026

### latest Posts

-   [Mid-Year 2026 Northern Nevada Commercial Real Estate Market Report Sky West Real Estate Services, Inc. Admin](https://skywestservices.com/2026/07/17/mid-year-2026-northern-nevada-commercial-real-estate-market-report/)
-   [Why Commercial Real Estate and Commercial Construction Go Hand in Hand Sky West Real Estate Services, Inc. Admin](https://skywestservices.com/2026/07/14/why-commercial-real-estate-and-commercial-construction-go-hand-in-hand/)
-   [Minden Gateway Center: Creating a Commercial Destination for a Growing Community Sky West Real Estate Services, Inc. Admin](https://skywestservices.com/2026/07/06/minden-gateway-center-creating-a-commercial-destination-for-a-growing-community/)

When people think about commercial real estate, they often picture buying, selling, or leasing properties. When they think about commercial construction, they imagine buildings, equipment, permits, and job sites. In reality, these two industries are deeply connected—and the most successful commercial projects happen when real estate and construction professionals work together from the very beginning. Whether you're purchasing land for a new development, renovating an existing building, expanding your business footprint, or preparing a property for a new tenant, understanding the relationship between commercial real estate and commercial construction can save significant time, money, and frustration.

## Commercial Real Estate Is More Than Finding a Building

A commercial real estate broker's role extends far beyond locating available properties. Experienced commercial real estate professionals help clients evaluate zoning requirements, development potential, access to utilities, traffic patterns, demographics, tenant demand, and long-term investment opportunities. They identify properties that not only meet today's needs but also support future growth. However, identifying the right property is only the first step. The next question is often: _"Can this property actually do what I need it to do?"_ That's where construction expertise becomes invaluable.

## The Construction Perspective Matters Early

Many business owners wait until after they have signed a lease or purchased a property before consulting a contractor. Unfortunately, that can lead to unexpected costs, project delays, permitting challenges, or costly redesigns. Bringing construction professionals into the conversation early allows owners and investors to understand:

-   Build-out costs
-   Tenant improvement requirements
-   Structural limitations
-   Utility and infrastructure needs
-   ADA compliance considerations
-   Permitting timelines
-   Expansion opportunities
-   Long-term maintenance requirements

Having this information before making a real estate decision can dramatically improve outcomes and prevent costly surprises.

## Ground-Up Development Requires Both Disciplines

Ground-up commercial development is perhaps the clearest example of why commercial real estate and commercial construction must work together. Before construction can begin, a project requires:

-   Site selection
-   Market analysis
-   Zoning review
-   Land acquisition
-   Utility planning
-   Entitlement research
-   Financial feasibility analysis

Once those pieces are in place, construction professionals transform a concept into a functioning asset. From retail centers and office buildings to industrial facilities and mixed-use developments, successful projects depend on both strategic real estate guidance and experienced construction execution.

## Tenant Improvements Can Create Value

Many commercial properties are not perfectly suited for their next occupant. A vacant warehouse may need office space added. A retail storefront may require significant interior renovations. An office building may need modernization to attract quality tenants. Tenant improvements allow property owners and businesses to customize spaces while increasing property value and marketability. The most successful tenant improvement projects begin with a clear understanding of both market demand and construction feasibility. Real estate professionals help identify what tenants want. # Why Exclusive Representation Matters in Commercial Real Estate

 [![](https://secure.gravatar.com/avatar/4c42b11208f0d6aee366c0cc59fad0f96efa9b3448330d9cf518e70308585960?s=32&d=mm&r=g) Jeffrey Lowden, MBA, Broker President of Sky West Real Estate Services](https://skywestservices.com/author/skywestrealestateservices/)

In commercial real estate, one of the biggest misconceptions clients have is believing that hiring multiple brokers creates competition that benefits them.

In reality, the opposite is usually true.

Whether you are leasing office space, purchasing an investment property, selling commercial assets, or searching for a retail location for your growing business, the most effective path is often working with one experienced commercial real estate professional who is fully committed to representing your interests.

At Sky West Real Estate Services, we have represented a wide variety of businesses and organizations across Northern Nevada, including behavioral health providers, wellness centers, breweries, fleet services, agricultural businesses, churches, fitness concepts, retailers, and professional offices.

The common denominator in successful transactions is almost always the same:

**Clear alignment, strong communication, and exclusive representation.**

## What Does “Exclusive Representation” Actually Mean?

An exclusive representation agreement means that one broker or brokerage is formally retained to represent your interests throughout a transaction.

That broker becomes your advocate, strategist, negotiator, researcher, marketer, and advisor.

Instead of multiple brokers competing against each other with fragmented communication and duplicated efforts, you gain a dedicated professional focused entirely on helping you achieve the best outcome possible.

In commercial real estate, this distinction matters more than many people realize.

## The “Multiple Brokers” Approach Often Backfires

Many business owners assume that putting several brokers on the same assignment increases exposure or accelerates results.

However, commercial real estate does not typically work that way.

Experienced brokers invest substantial time, money, market research, database access, negotiations, networking, marketing, and strategic planning into every assignment. When there is no commitment from the client, many brokers are understandably hesitant to dedicate their full resources to the opportunity.

Commercial real estate is highly relationship-driven.

The brokers who deliver the strongest results are often the ones who know they are trusted to carry the assignment from start to finish.

## Not All Commercial Real Estate Brokers Operate the Same Way

One of the biggest misconceptions in the industry is that all brokers have equal access, equal market knowledge, and equal capabilities simply because they use the same listing platforms.

The reality is far more nuanced.

Every property, tenant, investor, and market requires a customized strategy.

A successful commercial real estate transaction involves far more than posting a property online. It requires:

-   Market positioning
-   Pricing strategy
-   Negotiation expertise
-   Local relationships
-   Prospect outreach
-   Broker-to-broker networking
-   Marketing coordination
-   Lease structuring
-   Financial understanding
-   Long-term strategic thinking

At Sky West, we believe commercial real estate is intensely local. National exposure has value, but understanding the Reno market, the surrounding Northern Nevada communities, local economic trends, tenant movement, and off-market opportunities is what truly creates leverage for clients.

## The Work Clients Don’t Always See

One of the most overlooked aspects of commercial real estate is the amount of work that happens behind the scenes.

A single transaction may involve:

-   Property sourcing
-   Financial analysis
-   Market research
-   Multiple property tours
-   Broker coordination
-   Negotiation calls
-   Letters of Intent (LOIs)
-   Lease drafting and revisions
-   Concession negotiations
-   Due diligence coordination
-   Ongoing client advising over several months

In one tenant representation assignment discussed by Sky West, the transaction required nearly nine months of work, including multiple property showings, extensive negotiations, repeated revisions to agreements, and continuous coordination between parties.

From the outside, a completed transaction may appear straightforward.

Behind the scenes, however, successful deals often represent hundreds of hours of professional effort.

## Why Experience and Accountability Matter

Commercial real estate decisions can significantly impact a business’s future profitability and growth.

That is why **experience matters**.

The right broker helps clients avoid costly mistakes, structure stronger agreements, negotiate favorable terms, and position properties competitively within the market. Pricing strategy alone can dramatically affect how quickly a property leases or sells.

An experienced broker also provides accountability.

When a broker is exclusively retained, they are fully invested in protecting your interests and executing a strategy that aligns with your goals.

At Sky West, we believe our responsibility extends beyond simply “finding a property” or “listing a building.” We aim to become long-term advisors and advocates for our clients throughout every stage of the commercial real estate process.

## Commercial Real Estate Is About Relationships

The strongest transactions are built on trust.

Exclusive representation creates clarity, alignment, and commitment between broker and client, allowing both parties to work toward the same objective without confusion or competing agendas.

At Sky West Real Estate Services, we are proud to help businesses throughout Reno and Northern Nevada navigate commercial real estate with strategic guidance, local expertise, and hands-on representation.

If you are considering leasing, buying, selling, or evaluating commercial property opportunities, our team is available to assist with market analysis, lease review, investment strategy, and customized representation services.

## What Is Design Development?

Design development (DD) takes the schematic design (SD) one step further. Our architects take your input from the SD phase and translate it into a more detailed building plan. Within design development, we make final selections on specific products and furnishings, while preliminary engineering begins on the building’s various systems.

By the end of the design development stage, you will have a fully-fleshed blueprint ready to submit for permits and begin the bidding process.

### Architect-Directed Product Selection

A significant portion of design development is working with the client to make product selections. Our architects offer a pragmatic perspective during this task, and our guidance ensures that the project budget stays on track.

Allow us to assist you in exploring all the amenities available to you. More than that, allow us to help you analyze each one’s longevity, energy efficiency, value, and beauty. In design development, you can expect to give the final say on:

-   Flooring
-   Windows
-   Doors
-   Appliances
-   And more

This is an exciting time where you have free reign to add your personal flair to all these features. We’ll work closely with you to make sure every choice is judicious and will serve your best interests well into the future.

![Logo](https://skywestservices.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/1170/2026/03/fav.png)

### Interior Design Development

Bringing Details into Focus

Whatever details were lacking in the schematic design will be thoroughly addressed during design development. We go over our interior design with you to make sure everything from the entryway to the guest powder room gets your full stamp of approval.

At our firm, we like to think of design development as a focusing lens on a camera. At this stage, vague concepts are pulled into focus to reveal the details within. In DD, you work closely with the architect to identify and weed out potential flaws until only a robust design remains.

Upon DD completion, you’ll have a perfected:

-   Floor plan
-   Elevation plan
-   Section drawings with dimensions

With these documents solidified and in place, we shift our focus to the exciting task of construction.
