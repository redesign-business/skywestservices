## Completed Developments & Projects

### The Dunes Business Park
- Developed from the ground up in 2004 and 2005, The Dunes Business Park transformed raw land into a successful inline retail center located near the intersection of Cook Street and Washington Street in La Quinta, California.
- More than twenty years later, the center remains an active retail hub, today home to several recognizable businesses, including Goodwill, Schlotzsky's, and Floor & Decor.

### Sierra Vista Ranches
- Located along Eastlake Boulevard overlooking Little Washoe Lake, Sierra Vista Ranches was developed as a collection of thirteen 5-acre residential parcels.
- Today, each of the thirteen parcels has been purchased by individual homeowners who transformed the lots into custom homes overlooking Little Washoe Lake; what was once undeveloped land evolved into an established residential community.

### 954 E. 7th Street – Reno, NV
- Developed from the ground up, the approximately 5,000-square-foot facility was designed and constructed to accommodate the needs of Signature Stone, a growing building materials company.
- The development included the installation of 800-amp electrical service to support the tenant's specialized equipment and operational demands.

### Minden Gateway Center
- A strategic commercial development positioned to support continued growth in the Minden area, envisioned as an inline commercial center accommodating a variety of retail, professional, restaurant, and service-oriented businesses.

### 620 Ryland – Downtown Reno (investment marketing)
- Sky West marketed 620 Ryland with a multi-layered exposure strategy including digital marketing campaigns, professional property presentation, investor-focused messaging, social media and LinkedIn promotion, regional broker outreach, and market-driven content positioning.

### Security Circle Industrial Center (build-to-suit, now pre-leasing)
- A rare build-to-suit opportunity to lease 3.183 acres of land in North Reno with three proposed buildings.
- Building A: planned 24,000-square-foot facility divisible by four spaces, approximately 4,500 square feet each, plus an additional 2,000-square-foot mezzanine for each space.
- Building B: 18,000 square feet facility divisible into two spaces of 9,000 square feet each plus mezzanine.
- Building C: incubator space of 1,500 square feet, totaling up to 21,000 square feet.
- All building tenants can negotiate yard space in a half-acre fenced common yard.
- SF available: 1,500 sf - 63,600 sf; occupancy 2023.
